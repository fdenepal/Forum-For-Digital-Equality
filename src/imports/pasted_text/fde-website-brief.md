Design and build a premium, modern, editorial-style institutional website for **Forum for Digital Equality (FDE), Nepal**.

Website: https://digitalequality.org.np/

The website should feel like a **high-level international development organization / digital rights organization**, inspired by the visual quality, information hierarchy, editorial storytelling and professionalism of the current UNDP website, but it must NOT copy UNDP's branding, logo, layouts, colors, typography or exact components.

The website must have a completely distinct FDE identity.

==================================================
BRAND IDENTITY
==============

Organization:
Forum for Digital Equality

Short name:
FDE

Core brand color:
FDE ORANGE — #E8621A

Supporting colors:
Deep Charcoal — #111111
Warm Off White — #F7F5F2
White — #FFFFFF
Light Gray — #E9E9E9
Dark Orange — #B9470E

Use #E8621A as the main accent color throughout the website:

* buttons
* links
* small labels
* hover states
* statistics highlights
* section dividers
* selected navigation states
* important visual accents

Do NOT make the entire website orange.

The overall visual balance should be:
60% white/off-white
25% charcoal/black
10% photography/media
5% orange accent

Typography:
Use a modern premium sans-serif such as Inter, Manrope, or a similar contemporary grotesk typeface.

Typography should be:

* very large editorial headlines
* strong hierarchy
* short paragraphs
* generous whitespace
* bold numbers
* minimal decorative elements

Avoid:

* generic NGO website appearance
* excessive rounded cards
* excessive gradients
* excessive icons
* childish illustrations
* generic blue NGO styling
* excessive shadows
* cluttered layouts

==================================================
RESPONSIVE DESIGN
=================

Desktop:
Designed primarily for 1440px and 1920px screens.

Tablet:
Responsive two-column layouts.

Mobile:

* single-column editorial layouts
* video remains prominent
* navigation becomes hamburger
* statistics become vertical
* photography remains large
* typography scales elegantly
* no horizontal overflow

Maintain excellent spacing and readability.

==================================================
ACCESSIBILITY
=============

Target WCAG 2.1 AA principles.

Include:

* high contrast
* keyboard navigation
* visible focus states
* alt text for images
* captions/transcripts where needed for video
* semantic heading hierarchy
* accessible buttons
* readable font sizes
